Mechanism

One asset. Two tokens. Zero operators.

Covenant's CDP deposits yield-bearing collateral and mints two ERC-20s against it. The leveragecoin takes the multiplied exposure and pays the carry. The yieldcoin takes the carry and the principal protection.

01
Deposit yield-bearing collateral

Anyone deposits shMON — or MON that auto-wraps — into a Covenant vault. The vault holds the shMON as productive collateral and continues to earn the full 12.78% base APR from Monad staking.

02
Mint the split ERC-20s

Against every unit of shMON, the vault mints a matched pair: one shMONxN.{usd,MON} leveragecoin with N× the price and yield exposure, and one {USD,MON}.b.shMON yieldcoin that holds the (N−1)× debt claim.

03
Carry flows one direction

Every block, the leveragecoin side accrues a borrow rate on the debt — denominated in USD or MON depending on the market. That interest streams straight to the yieldcoin, which earns it as passive APR.
